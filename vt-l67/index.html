First-start sequence 6 steps
  1. Bonnet open, extinguisher nearby, helper watching for leaks.
  2. Key ON first. Check for fuel leaks before cranking.
  3. Crank only after oil priming. Stop for grinding or heavy cranking.
  4. Oil warning must go out promptly after start. If not, shut down.
  5. Keep revs low. Watch temperature, charging, belts, smoke and leaks.
  6. Shut down for knock, grinding, fuel leak, coolant dump or overheating.
